# Watch Later — Launch Video Plan

## Overview
- **Product**: Watch Later (Android APK & Centralized Web Dashboard)
- **Concept**: "The Death of Scattered Saves"
- **Duration**: 20 seconds (Cinematic, Punchy, Physical)
- **Visual Tone**: Deep midnight black (#090909), crisp white typography (#ffffff), subtle titanium accents (#9b9b9b), fluid micro-particles.
- **Narrative Arc**:
  1. **Beat 1: The Problem (0-4s)** — "You save reels to watch later. You never watch them." Scattered saves, forgotten bookmarks, digital hoarder guilt.
  2. **Beat 2: The Action (4-8s)** — "Share, don't save." Instagram share sheet $\rightarrow$ One tap to Watch Later $\rightarrow$ Silent 0-UI capture.
  3. **Beat 3: The Engine (8-13s)** — Background audio transcription, caption parsing, and automatic taxonomy sorting into clean niche pillars.
  4. **Beat 4: The Payoff (13-17s)** — A curated, searchable library. Watch $\rightarrow$ Auto-cleared from queue. Zero backlog forever.
  5. **Beat 5: The Call to Action (17-20s)** — "Never lose a reel worth revisiting." Download Android App (APK).
