# Brag Plan: Watch Later

## What is this app?
Watch Later is an Android app and curated knowledge library that intercepts Instagram reels via the native share sheet with zero popups, transcribes audio and captions, organizes them into searchable pillars, and auto-purges them once watched.

## The angle
Brutally honest recognition of the universal human habit: saving dozens of reels to watch later and never touching them again. Instead of a chronological junk drawer or DM thread to yourself, Watch Later turns reels into a self-cleaning, searchable personal knowledge base.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on obsidian background: "Saved reels go to die." Counter ticking through the reality: "842 Saved Posts (Never Re-opened)" and "DMs to yourself: 'watch this later'".

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Silent Intercept**: Tap share in Instagram → Watch Later captures the reel in the background with 0 UI popups. You never leave your feed.
- **Substance Extraction**: Audio speech-to-text + transcript indexing + automated category clustering into focused pillars (Systems & Architecture, Kinetic UI, Culinary, Mobility).
- **The Self-Purging Queue**: The anti-hoarding guarantee — once you watch a reel, it disappears forever. No stale backlog, no guilt.

## Outro / punchline
"Never lose a reel worth revisiting." Download the native Android App (APK) today. Direct download · Free.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: Tap native Android share sheet inside Instagram.
2. Key Action: Silent intercept in ~3 seconds; audio transcribed and categorized into curated pillars.
3. Result: Clean web & mobile library; completed reels auto-purge upon watching.
